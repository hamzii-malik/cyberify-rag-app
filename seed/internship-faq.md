# Cyberify Internship Program — FAQ

This document answers common questions from interns joining the Cyberify training program.

The internship runs for eight weeks. Interns work in small groups of three to four people, and each group is paired with a mentor from the engineering team.

Attendance is tracked daily. Interns are expected to be online or on-site by 9:30 a.m. on weekdays. More than three unexcused absences may result in removal from the program.

Interns receive a certificate of completion at the end of the eight weeks, provided they have finished at least 80 percent of the assigned projects. Top-performing interns may be offered a full-time position or a paid extension.

Stipends are paid monthly, on the last working day of each month, directly to the bank account provided during onboarding.
