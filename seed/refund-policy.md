# Cyberify Refund Policy

We want you to be happy with every purchase. This document explains how refunds and returns work.

Undamaged items may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as the original packaging is intact and the item has not been used.

Damaged items may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Please include photos of the damage when you contact support, so we can process the refund faster.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the returned item. Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the item arrived damaged or defective.

Digital products and gift cards are not eligible for refunds once delivered.
